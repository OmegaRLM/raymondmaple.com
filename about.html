Developer · Educator · Futurist

I'm a developer and educator with over 20 years of experience building interactive experiences. My work lives at the intersection of game development, AI, XR, and simulations — creating things that immerse and entertain people.

I spent the core of my career at WayForward Technologies and Disney / Avalanche Software, shipping titles across handheld, console, and PC. At Avalanche I contributed to the Disney Infinity franchise, Epic Mickey, Cars, Toy Story, and more.

Outside of games, I teach, write, and build tools at the edges of what's possible with code, sound, and visuals.
